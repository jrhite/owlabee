FAQ

How do I get opportunities for my own keywords?

Run Grants.gov API & SAM.gov Scraper — Federal Grants Feed with your keywords. Add "samGov" to sources with your free SAM.gov key for federal contract notices, and turn on onlyNewSinceLastRun on a daily schedule to receive only what is new or changed.

Which state and local datasets are covered?

Nineteen official open-data datasets (Los Angeles, New York City, Montgomery County MD, Mesa AZ, Cook County IL, Illinois, Delaware, California, New York SUCF, TxDOT, Baton Rouge, San Diego County and the national E-Rate Form 470 file). The Grants.gov API guide lists them.

Can it rank opportunities for my organization?

Yes, a separate Actor does that: Grant Matching & RFP Bid/No-Bid Scorer (AI) — Grants.gov scores each opportunity against a profile of your organization and explains the score.
