Privacy

What owlabee.com collects. No accounts, no forms, no ads.

Visit statistics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which guides are read. It records things like the pages you view, how you arrived (a search, a link, or directly), whether you follow a link to an Actor on Apify Store, your approximate location at country or city level, and your device and browser type. Google does not give owlabee your IP address.

Advertising features are turned off. If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Google Analytics runs in a cookieless mode and sets no analytics cookies. Elsewhere it sets first-party cookies (named _ga) to tell new visits from return ones.

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. You can opt out on every site with Google's opt-out browser add-on, or by blocking scripts from googletagmanager.com.

Fonts and hosting

The fonts (Jost and Cormorant Garamond) load from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, handles the requests your browser makes and keeps routine logs to run and protect the service.

Running an Actor

The Actors themselves run on Apify, under your own Apify account. What you send them and what they return is covered by Apify's privacy policy, not this site.
